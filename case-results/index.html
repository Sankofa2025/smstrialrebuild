About Case Results in Georgia

What factors influence a case's value?
Liability strength, the severity and permanence of injuries, medical expenses (past and future), lost income, insurance coverage, and the credibility of evidence and witnesses all shape a recovery.
Do these results guarantee my outcome?
No. Every case turns on its own facts and law. Prior results do not guarantee or predict a similar outcome in any future matter.
Why do trial-ready firms recover more?
Insurers evaluate whether a firm will actually try a case. A documented record of verdicts signals that lowball offers will be rejected — which drives fairer settlements.
